## Decided
The user requested an English contribution agreement and a README link on 2026-10-22. CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md version 0.1 implements section 3 of the existing custom LICENSE, which remains unchanged. Contributors retain ownership. The agreement grants the maintainer rights to modify, distribute, sublicense and commercially exploit covered contributions, with narrowly scoped patent permissions and authorization requirements for employers, co-authors and third-party material.

## Tried and ruled out
A pull request, merge or commit sign-off alone is not assent to these separate terms. The acceptance record identifies the agreement's immutable revision and the submitted contribution revisions. Later changes need updated or additional acceptance; the document does not silently bind past or future submissions. Do not turn the CLA into general public redistribution rights or into a copyright assignment. The source-available project licence continues to govern users.

## Watch out
This is a drafted agreement, not a signed agreement or legal validation. No contributor has accepted it as a result of writing the file. The maintainer must retain the explicit acceptance and authority evidence, acknowledge completeness before incorporation, and avoid committing completed signature records/private contact details. No CLA bot, merge gate, jurisdiction or private contact address was invented. README links and document structure were checked; runtime tests do not validate a legal agreement. The former learning that no agreement existed describes the earlier state only.
