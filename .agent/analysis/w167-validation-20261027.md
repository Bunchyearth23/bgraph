# W-167 — validation of the residual caption/label collisions (27 October 2026)

Scope: the remainder of `W-167` — every caption/label collision still reported
today — and the two engine-caused ones found by the W-168 agent's replay.

**Outcome.** Twelve residual warnings were recorded on 20 October: **eleven
caption/label collisions** and one declared-`size` warning. All eleven are
classified below with the geometry that decides them. **Two were engine-caused
and are fixed** (`src/labels.rs`): the boundary-event caption that grazed its
host task, and the caption that a declared `label-position` left lying across a
flow. Eight are the author's own declarations and are still reported, exactly as
before. **No diagnostic was weakened**: every residual report that remains is
still emitted, and the two that disappeared disappeared because the caption
*moved*.

**Class (ii) — a declared offset overridden wrongly — occurs nowhere.** The two
pinned captions are reproduced identically before and after the change (§3), and
the W-167 contract's own case (`label-offset: 0 0` keeping the pictured
collision) still reports.

**One point of disagreement with the W-168 agent, stated openly.** Its replay
classifies the last row as "author-declared" — a declared `label-position` on an
event whose flow leaves to the right — and offers `label-position: bottom` or a
declared `label-offset` as the author's fix. That reading is defensible, and
both remedies work. This work takes the other reading: `label-position` names
the *side* ("Pin an external BPMN event or gateway caption to this side",
`docs/reference.md`), not the point along it, and text lying across a wire is a
defect rather than a warning. The fix keeps the side exactly where the author
put it and only steps the caption along it, so the author's declaration is
honoured either way; if the owner prefers the W-168 reading, the change to
revert is the slide in §2.3 and the side filter in §2.2, and the report comes
back.

The eight author-declared reports are the author's to move, and the engine says
so: `op_state` with `label-offset: 0 -82` and `c_ack` with `0 -68` each silence
their own report and leave the other one standing (verified on copies of
`06-boundary-in-context.bd`; the fixtures themselves were not edited).

## 2. The change — `src/labels.rs`

Three parts, all inside the file this work item owns.

1. **The host's edge is a candidate anchor** (the patch handed over by the
   W-168 agent). A boundary event hangs half over the task it is attached to,
   so "beside the event, bottom-aligned" puts the caption's top a hair *above*
   the task's edge. Candidates anchored on `host.rect.bottom()` clear the box.
   The caption of `e` now starts exactly at the task's bottom edge (300.0,
   was 299.72): the report is gone because the position changed.
2. **A declared *side* keeps the side; only the point along it is the
   engine's.** `label-position` names the side of the shape the caption belongs
   on. The candidates are now filtered to that side (`on_side`), so the caption
   of `got` stays to the right of its event.
3. **A caption drawn over a flow steps along that side.** The cost now keeps
   the flow component apart from the box component: a caption whose padded box
   grazes a box is a warning, a caption whose *ink* lies across a wire is a
   defect. Only the latter earns the bounded slide (six steps of half the
   caption's depth plus its clearance). The caption of `got` steps up 13.14
   units and clears the wire by 3.

**A declared `label-offset` is never overridden.** The pin test is now
`pinned && style.label_offset.is_some()`: an explicit offset (zero included)
still skips the refinement entirely, and a caption whose side is declared but
whose offset is not may only move *along* that side.

## 3. Tests

Four tests in `src/labels.rs` (`mod tests`), each of which fails before its
fix and passes after:

- `a_boundary_event_caption_clears_the_task_it_hangs_on` — no report for `e`,
  and the caption's box starts at the host task's bottom edge.
  *Fails before*: "the caption of a boundary event must not overlap the task it
  hangs on" (the warning is reported).
- `a_pinned_boundary_caption_still_reports_its_collision` — the same source
  with `label-offset: 0 0` still reports: the fix moved the caption, it did not
  silence the diagnostic.
- `a_caption_pinned_to_a_side_steps_along_it_to_clear_the_flow` — no report
  for `got`, the caption is still right of its event, and no routed segment
  crosses it.
  *Fails before*: the warning is reported.
- `a_pinned_caption_offset_wins_over_the_step_along_the_side` — with
  `label-offset: 0 0` the caption stays exactly at the declared position and
  the collision is still reported.

The two pinned captions in `06-boundary-in-context.bd` are **byte-identical**
before and after the change: the annotation positions are
`("Pipeline Running", 2476.0, 133.2)`, `("(sondage /pipelines)", 2476.0,
146.46)`, `("DeployAck", 2266.0, 533.2)` in both runs.
