## 7. User checklist — VS Code / VSCodium and Zed, after a reload

Both editors show the same SVG; the list is what to look at, per family.

**Reload first.** VS Code/VSCodium: *Developer: Reload Window*. Zed: restart
Zed (the extension is a dev extension).

**A. Declared layouts** — free, class, block, er, state, harness, bpmn,
network, requirements.
1. Open the archetype's own example (the `examples/` gallery) and add
   `legend { title: "Key"  entry "One" .live }` at the end.
2. The key must appear **below the whole drawing**, with clear white space
   between the last box and the key — not beside a box, not on top of one.
3. The title must be on its own line above the entries; each entry has a
   small square **to the left of** its text.
4. The square must be the colour of `.live`, not of the diagram's default node
   fill.
5. Now change it to `legend below <some box>`: the key must sit **under that
   box**, aligned to its left edge, and the diagram must not otherwise move.
6. `legend at (200, 500)`: the key must land exactly there, and the page must
   grow to include it.

**B. Managed charts** — pie, gantt, timeline, sequence, xy, bar, sankey, wave,
quadrant, treemap, packet, bytefield, gitgraph, orgchart, mindmap, treeview,
venn, rack, radar, journey, kanban.
1. A bare `legend { … }` must appear **below the chart**, not beside it.
2. `legend below x` must be refused with the sentence *"`type pie` manages its
   own geometry…"* — in the Problems panel, not silently.
3. `legend at (x, y)` must be honoured exactly.

**C. The six with a chart key of their own** — pie, bar, xy, radar, journey,
venn.
1. **Two** keys must be visible: the chart's own (beside the plot, naming its
   series or sets) and the block's (below the drawing). Neither may replace
   the other.
2. The chart's key must not move when the block is added; only the page
   heading may re-centre.

**D. Ink and background.**
1. On a dark example (`#0b1120`) the key text must be pale, not dark.
2. On a light example (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`) it must be near-black.
3. `font-color: #facc15` inside the block must override it.

**E. Legacy.** A style with `legend: "Healthy"` plus a bare `legend` must still
produce the same key.

**F. Editor specifics.** In both editors, `legend {`, `entry "…"` and `title:`
must highlight as keywords/strings, and the legend block must not break
indentation inside `{ }`.
